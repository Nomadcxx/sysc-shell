## Points the audit must confirm beyond the spec walk

1. **Decode strictness end to end** (`weather/client.go`): every new field —
   current apparent/is_day/humidity/wind/uv, daily uv/precip, hourly block,
   root elevation/timezone — is nil when the body omits or nulls it, never a
   silent zero. The optional daily/hourly arrays *pad* the required columns
   rather than truncate (`fillDaily`/`fillHourly`), and a short array covers
   only the rows it has. Confirm the daily early-return cannot short-circuit
   the hourly block again (`ff463f8` fixed exactly that; is the structure safe
   against the next field?).
2. **Vocabulary completeness**: exactly one WMO→glyph mapping
   (`render.IconRune`/`WeatherIcon`) and one word table
   (`render.WeatherCondition`) survive. Grep for `sunny`,
   `partly_cloudy_day`, `conditionWords`, `ccWeatherIcon` remnants. The
   plugin's `Condition()` is a protocol consumer and deliberately keeps its own
   wording (D1) — not a finding.
3. **The three-state rule on every surface** (D9): bar text, bar tooltip,
   panel hero, and the control-centre page each distinguish placeholder
   (normal tone), never-observed failure (error tone), and stale-with-age.
   The control-centre page ignored `FailedSince` before this slice.
4. **Ladder conformance**: the token scan
   (`TestSurfaceSourcesCarryNoLegacyVisuals`) is necessary but not sufficient.
   Read the arithmetic beside every derived value in `weatherpanel.go` —
   `bodyH`, `leftW`, `rightW`, `weatherHeaderHeight`, `weatherRowHeight` — and
   confirm each traces to `theme.Metrics` or a named rung, and that
   `IconHero` (56/64/80/92/104 across the five density rows) is plausible
   against the measured hero proportion (2.2 × `controlHeightLg` ≈ 97 px,
   research doc).
5. **Font artefact integrity**: `internal/render/icons/sysc-icons.ttf` was
   regenerated by `build.py` with eight new glyphs (uniE028–uniE02F). Confirm
   the builder inventory, `iconfont.go`'s constants and the `iconNames` map
   are in step, and the ink tests
   (`TestNightGlyphsCarryInk`, `TestWeatherDetailGlyphsCarryInk`) pass.
6. **Geocoding degradation** (`weather/geocoding.go`, `SetCity`, fetch): a
   geocode failure follows the normal failure/backoff path, the cached
   resolution survives forecast retries, a city change re-resolves, and the
   config's exactly-one-of rule (`city` xor `latitude`+`longitude`) cannot be
   bypassed through `write.go`'s round trip.
7. **Panel identity invariants**: `PanelWeather` is enum-end; the blur walk
   (`TestEveryPanelRequestsABackdrop`) covers it; `panelSpec` needs no
   weather special case; the dispatch actually reaches `weatherTree` (the
   placeholder dispatch was a real hole once — `9ca563f` fixed it and pinned
   it; confirm nothing similar is left).
