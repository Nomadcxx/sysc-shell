Six upgrades for the sysc bar

I read the bar code of DankMaterialShell, Noctalia v5 and Caelestia (current main of each, 20–25 September 2026) and pulled out what they do that sysc-shell doesn't yet. Every scene below uses sysc's real bar geometry (40 px body, 28 px pills, 12 px radius, 4 px gap) and the centre pill from PR #13.

The blur answer. Both Noctalia and DMS get blur from the compositor, not by blurring pixels themselves. They publish the bar's shape through ext-background-effect-v1, and Niri 26.04+ blurs whatever is behind it on the GPU, live, at no cost to the shell. sysc-shell's panel blur (one screen capture per open, blurred on the CPU) was the right call before Niri had this. For the bar it's the wrong tool, because the bar is always on screen.

Fallback. On an older Niri, the bar can keep today's opaque look, or blur the wallpaper image sysc-shell already owns. A docked bar with a reserved zone only ever sits over the wallpaper.

The six, one at a time

Suggested order

1 first. The compositor-blur binding is the foundation for 2 and for translucent panels later, and it's the change you'll notice most. Check niri --version is 26.04 or later.

Then 3 and 5. Each is self-contained. Segments need per-corner radii, which 4 and a future hanging tab also need.

4 and 6 last. Both are bigger. 4 changes the bar's surface geometry, and 6 routes notifications, media and the volume OSD into the centre pill.
